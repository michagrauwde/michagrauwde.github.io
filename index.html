About

I research how AI systems impact meta-cognition. In particular, I explore the implications of large language models for human reasoning and decision-making. I look at how these systems impact and can be designed to support human thinking particularly reflection the context of deliberation. This page collects a selection of my work. My blog is where I explore questions on a multitude of topics such as the role of specialization in science whereas I also have a collection of my scholarly work here. More is on my blog and in my papers.

More of my writing is on my blog, and the full list of papers is here.
